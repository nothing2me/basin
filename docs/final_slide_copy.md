# Final slide copy

## What BASIN contributes

BASIN turns public rainfall observations and explicit assumptions into a transparent scenario shortlist for expert review. Its strongest contribution is traceability: each selected scenario can be followed from point-gauge inputs through ranking, internal review and a replayable handoff bundle.

BASIN is a pre-model screening tool for South Texas planning discussions. It does not provide an operational water-supply forecast or hydrologic validation.
