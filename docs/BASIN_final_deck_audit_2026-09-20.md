## Overall judgment

The deck has a strong and recognizable visual identity, a clear narrative sequence, readable large type, and good use of actual product screenshots. The vocabulary section is particularly effective for a mixed audience. The primary weakness is completion and synchronization: several slides still contain production placeholders, outdated claims, old speaker notes, or screenshots from a different footprint than the live-demo plan.

The deck is not ready to freeze in its current form. It tracks the revised script conceptually, but it remains one content revision behind it.
