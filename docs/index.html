03. Physical Governing Equations & Mass Conservation Constraints

Specification: Finite-Difference Reservoir Continuity
PHYSICS CORE 01

Finite-Difference Continuity

Storage volume at day t+1 is governed strictly by the volumetric continuity equation across discrete time steps:

S(t+1) = S(t) + I(t) - E(S(t)) - R(t) - W(t) S = Storage Volume (Acre-Feet)
I = Gage Inflow (Acre-Feet/day)
E = Net Surface Evaporative Loss
R = Pass-Through Releases
W = Raw Water Municipal/Industrial Diversions

Compiled in deterministic C/NumPy kernels. The language model is forbidden from performing arithmetic.

PHYSICS CORE 02

Elevation-Area-Capacity Polynomials

Net evaporation accounts for instantaneous lake surface contraction via quadratic elevation-area polynomials:

E(t) = A(S(t)) * [e_pan * k_c - P(t)] A(S) = c_0 + c_1 * S + c_2 * S^2 A(S) = Dynamic Surface Area (Acres)
e_pan = NOAA Pan Evaporation Rate
k_c = Monthly Lake-to-Pan Coefficient
P(t) = Surface Direct Precipitation

Eliminates the common Excel spreadsheet error where evaporative losses are computed against static surface areas during drawdowns.

PHYSICS CORE 03

8D K-Means Climate Clustering

Clusters 70+ years of historical Texas climate and streamgage records across 8 standardized physical dimensions:

Vector X = [PDSI, SPI_90, Q_pctl, SM_idx, E_demand, T_anom, dS_dt, S_up] 1. Palmer Drought Severity Index (PDSI)
2. 90-Day Standardized Precipitation Index

4. Root-Zone Soil Moisture Deficit
