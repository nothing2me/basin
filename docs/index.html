Engineering Fundamentals

Rigorous hydrologic physics. Zero hallucinations.

General-purpose AI models hallucinate arithmetic and fail conservation laws. BASIN is engineered specifically for mission-critical municipal water systems.

01 / DETERMINISTIC CONTINUITY

Strict Mass Conservation

The AI model never computes reservoir volumes. All storage projections execute in compiled C/NumPy finite-difference kernels where mass conservation is guaranteed: S(t+1) = S(t) + I(t) - E(S(t)) - R(t) - W(t)

02 / AIR-GAPPED PRIVACY

100% On-Device Execution

Municipal water data and drought contingency plans never leave your infrastructure. BASIN operates completely offline with zero telemetry, zero cloud APIs, and zero subscription requirements.

03 / REGULATORY TRANSPARENCY

Tamper-Evident Decision Briefs

Export cryptographic SHA-256 signed executive PDF briefs for city councils, river authority boards, and TCEQ regulatory hearings. Fully compliant with Texas Occupations Code § 1001 screening standards.
