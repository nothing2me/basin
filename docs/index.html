Engineering Fundamentals

Rigorous hydrologic physics. Zero calculation hallucinations.

General-purpose LLMs hallucinate numbers and violate conservation laws. In BASIN, all mathematical calculations and simulations are strictly quarantined within deterministic, vectorized Python and NumPy kernels. The optional local AI assistant operates exclusively through read-only inspection tools and deterministic routing—it never invents or computes numbers.

01 / DETERMINISTIC CONTINUITY

Strict Mass Conservation

The AI model never computes reservoir volumes. All storage projections execute in vectorized NumPy finite-difference kernels where mass conservation is guaranteed: S(t+1) = S(t) + I(t) - E(S(t)) - R(t) - W(t)

02 / AIR-GAPPED PRIVACY

100% On-Device Execution

Municipal water data and drought contingency plans never leave your infrastructure. BASIN operates completely offline with zero telemetry, zero cloud APIs, and zero subscription requirements.

03 / REGULATORY TRANSPARENCY

Tamper-Evident Decision Briefs

Export a SHA-256 hash-checked replay bundle with a companion PDF for internal planning discussions and pre-model technical handoff.
